The owner's new direction removes speed controls. The game should move automatically: readable conversations and orders, brisk empty stretches, and one “Until something happens” action when nothing needs the player. It must stop at the next event and preserve existing saves and replays.

The implementation holds the clock while the conversation or an inspection is open. Close resumes it. Undelivered orders receive 0.6 game minutes per real second; walking and work run at 6. New speech, people and orders stop a fast batch at their exact minute and receive reading time. Journal-only background chatter does not repeatedly hold automatic playback. The wait action stops on the first new line, person, order or night page. It steps the unchanged simulation one minute at a time.
