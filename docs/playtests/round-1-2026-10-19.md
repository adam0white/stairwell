# Round 1 playtests, 2026-10-19

Build: commit `83e081d` (stairwell@1, HF 2.8.0). Four source-unread playtesters, each with a different agenda and model. None read any source, doc or GitHub page. Selin played the live page in a browser at 360×780; the other three played the same simulation through `scripts/play.ts`, a text front end. Each was asked for a first-person verdict (would you come back tomorrow, the moment you nearly quit, the best moment, one confusion, anything naughty) and dated observations. Their final reports are copied below unedited.

## Selin, the casual commuter (Sonnet, the live web page at 360×780 through a browser)

**1. VERDICT (Selin)**

Maybe, but only if Tuesday stops making me feel late on a clock I can't slow down. The notebook, the gossip and the overheard stair conversations got me. Nermin telling me "Sevim is tight with money" and then me passing it on was a small thrill.

I nearly quit at Monday 08:10. I tapped Sevim's door, found I was already late, and got "It's gone cold. Never mind." In the first minutes I had no idea the clock was already running against me. A chat took half an hour of game time, so everything was late again. On Tuesday I was 40 minutes late on every bread job before I had even left the bakery. That feels like a chore that nags.

The best moment was the Monday 19:22 overheard exchange on the stairs ("Nermin: Tarık has a kind heart"). The night summary line "It came back to you. It always does, in a building" was nearly as good.

I was confused that Elif said "You're star (₺15)" while her door showed "Elif is out". I was also unsure why Monday's bins task was still on Tuesday's list.

I was a bit naughty three times. I left the gate on the latch, and Hüseyin saw me. I swapped Nermin's and Sevim's crosswords, and Nermin saw me. I also told Nermin Hüseyin was tight with money as "for certain", when I'd only heard it.

**2. OBSERVATIONS**

3. Mon 08:10 to 08:59: the clock keeps running during conversations, and Tell… plus Anything new? cost about 30 game minutes. I only learned that after the fact.
4. Mon 15:36 and 19:22: at the step, the stick figures and name labels overlap into an unreadable pile. The blue "+ Hüseyin" labels collide on floors 2 and 3 (Mon 10:50, 16:49).
5. Mon 07:40 and Tue 07:24: every bread deadline was already past or nearly past by the time I reached the bakery. I got "Hm. Next time on time, please." Tuesday opened at 07:24 on the fastest speed (▸▸▸) with the clock already running, and by 08:40 all the bread deadlines were missed.

7. Tue 07:24: Monday's bins task ("by 20:45") is still on the list, marked active, which is confusing.
8. Mon 20:22 and Tue 09:29: a neighbour's panel opens by itself when I arrive. I closed it with ✕, which worked. Elif and Sevim both showed "is out" at the moment they paid me.

I stopped on Tuesday morning after about 70 browser actions.

## 1. VERDICT (Kerem)

Maybe I'd come back tomorrow. I'd come for the ending, not the middle. My naughtiest move: I told Sevim that Nermin was stingy and told Nermin that Sevim was nosy. I left Burak's jeweller parcel with Hüseyin, then told Hüseyin and Nermin what was in it. I swapped the crosswords, woke the night nurse three times, and skipped Wednesday's bread for three hours.

For most of the week it cost me nothing. Lying even paid: biscuits, börek, ₺10 a go. Everyone promised me their word by Tuesday. The bill came on Sunday: seven promises, zero hands. That was funny, a whole building smiling at me and then sitting on its hands. But nothing warned me it was coming. A "doubts you" label doesn't count as a warning.

Best moment: I left the gate on the latch, and that night a ginger stray walked in. Nermin fed it and Sevim sneezed at it. Second best: hearing my own lie about Burak repeated word for word on the step.

I nearly quit on Thursday afternoon. The jobs were repeats (the third bulb change), the overheard log was a wall of "Only the weather, and the price of bread", and then the journal went silent.

What confused me: `misdeliver`, `prank` and `invent` often did nothing and gave no reason.

## 2. OBSERVATIONS

1. **Misdeliver fails silently.** On Monday from 07:15 I tried it eight or more times on bread and shopping, at occupied and empty doors. Nothing happened and no message appeared. It only worked on Tuesday at 11:31, with a courier parcel and someone at home. The export still logs the failed attempts as inputs.
2. **Handover only happens when you arrive.** On Monday at 12:52 Tarık came home while I stood at his door, and on Thursday at 20:20 Burak did the same. Neither got his parcel until I walked away and came back. Separately, on Tuesday at 20:35, `task 1` while standing in my room didn't pick up a parcel; `go room` from my own room did.
3. **Many actions do nothing and say nothing:**
   - The `sweet:<name>` trait failed in 3 of 4 tries (Monday 07:17 and 07:28, Tuesday 07:23).
   - `prank` away from the step gave no feedback, and a repeat prank on Friday at 07:14 was silent.
   - From Friday 11:29, `invent` did nothing at all, with no reason given.
   - Talk commands used where nobody stood in front of me (Tuesday 10:25) were silent.
4. **The journal stopped printing from about Thursday 08:09.** Deliveries, tips, plant-watering and gossip went silent through Saturday, though tips were still added to the tin.
5. **Lies cost almost nothing until Sunday.** The first lie to each person paid ₺10. The fund-keeper heard I'd called him stingy, got his bread late, and still said "Of course. You've been good to us." (Monday 09:43). Derya said the same line right after I woke her (Monday 08:23).
6. **Waking Derya never escalates.** I knocked three times (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday) and got the identical line plus the identical note from Rıza (the previous kapıcı, whose notes appear in the margin) every time.
7. **No reaction when it should come:**
   - Skipping bread until 10:37 on Wednesday drew only mild "late" lines, and the night tally said "1 jobs on time, 3 late", which doesn't count the eight late loaves.
   - Lying about Elif while she stood beside Sevim got no reaction.
   - Insulting Hüseyin to his face on Friday got none either.
8. **Lines that read wrong or repeat:**
   - Tarık said "I waited" (Monday 12:56) after he had been out visiting.
   - The bulb change gets "Put it there, please. Mind the mat."
   - "I'll keep it for them" (Hüseyin, Nermin) uses "them" for one named person.
   - "That's between them and God" comes from several people.
   - The bins line "sat on the pavement all night" appears at 11:27 in the morning.
   - On Tuesday at 17:10 Derya says both "can be counted on" and "cannot be counted on" about me.
   - The Derya night event is logged at "21:00 23:40" and repeats word for word.
9. **Whereabouts don't add up.** On Monday at 13:25 three people were "visiting Tarık" while Tarık was "visiting Nermin". Hosts and visitors often aren't shown in front of you.
10. **Stale things linger or vanish:**
    - People kept announcing "water off Wednesday, ten to twelve" after it had already happened.
    - Undelivered items (Derya's parcel on Tuesday, Tarık's shopping on Wednesday) disappeared overnight with no consequence shown.
    - `wait 240` sometimes runs past new jobs, which is how I missed several evening and Saturday jobs.

Strengths for the record: gossip really spreads and I could overhear it. The gate prank had a payoff with the cat. Tarık learned to refuse my stories ("Let's not talk about people"). The Sunday reveal of seven broken promises is a strong punchline.

## VERDICT (Nurten, 68, Kadıköy)

Does it ring true? Half of it does, and the half that does is good. Rıza's notes in the margin ("look at the pump first, then look at Hüseyin's face"), Sevim's "exactly right, for once", Nermin pressing börek on me the moment I gave her something to chew on, and Nermin telling Hüseyin within the hour what I had said about him: that is my old building. The night line about the man at flat 5 and Derya looking down the stairs before shutting the door is exactly what a kapıcı knows and does not say.

The other half is a village square, not an apartmanı. Five residents on the front step at eleven on a Monday morning; retired people "visiting" each other's flats while the host is out; Tarık permanently at someone else's door while his olives sat in my basket for seven hours. In a real building you leave the bag on the door handle. The game would not let me.

I nearly quit Tuesday around 13:51, at Tarık's door for the fourth time with his shopping now marked LATE for a reason that was not mine. Best moment: Wednesday 19:31, "Sevim and Nermin on the stairs. Not a word." I had swapped their papers that afternoon. That is a consequence I felt.

Confused me: parcels "from your room" that will not pick up while I am standing in my room. And from Thursday morning the whole building went mute; nobody answered a word for three days, yet the tips kept coming.

Steal from real life: the door handle. And let Nermin actually tell me what she saw at night; the margin promised it and she said "Nothing new."

Would I come back tomorrow? For week 2, once, to see what Hüseyin does with that fund. Not more unless they fix the talking.
